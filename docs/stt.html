No quality winner is declared yet. Speed proves that a model can finish quickly; it does not prove that it preserved ClickHouse, RAG or a vendor name. The ranking will appear only after a human has confirmed the same time-bounded term slots for every variant.

What “better” means here

  1. Four frozen 15-minute technical segments have the same SHA-256 source authority for every run.
  2. Every transcript must cover the complete duration using the same 25-second, zero-overlap chunk plan; model or device substitutions are rejected.
  3. Systems may propose candidate terms, but cannot grade themselves. A human listens to bounded intervals and creates the reference authority.
  4. The primary score is exact Latin-script recall of the confirmed IT terms. Cyrillic transliteration, a plausible wrong Latin spelling and an omission remain distinct errors.
  5. Runtime is a tie-break only. A fast model cannot win by turning the important word into a plausible but wrong one.

Why these eight variants

Whisper baseline

faster-whisper small

Low-cost local baseline: it establishes what the larger Whisper variants actually buy.

Whisper frontier

faster-whisper large-v3-turbo

Tests the likely speed/accuracy frontier against domain spellings instead of assuming “turbo” is free.

Whisper ceiling

faster-whisper large-v3

Largest Whisper baseline for code-switched terms.

Russian-native

GigaAM v3 e2e RNNT

Official Russian end-to-end RNN-T path, pinned by source commit and checkpoint hash.

Independent CTC

Meta MMS-1B Russian adapter

Separates broad Russian coverage from the ability to retain English IT terminology.

LLM-style ASR

Qwen3-ASR 0.6B

Candidate designed to recognise product and vendor names inside Russian/English code-switching.

Joint model

MOSS Transcribe Diarize 0.9B

Tests whether a multilingual GPU-native model with speaker modelling helps podcast-like meeting audio.

Operational baseline

Vosk Russian 0.42

Explicit CPU-only offline baseline on a large machine: included to measure whether simple local speech recognition is operationally sufficient.
